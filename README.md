# Kruger Tick List

A safari sightings tick list for Kruger National Park: male and female pictures of each animal, Big 5 / Ugly 5 / Little 5 filters, likely-to-see ordering for your camp, lodge or safari provider, and a trip plan (activity, time of day, weather).

## Use it

Open **https://peshnz.github.io/kruger-tick-list/** on your phone and install it:

- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install app** under the title, or ⋮ → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

After the first open it works fully offline. Ticks, location, plan and your own photos are saved on the phone.

## Updating

Change the files, then rebuild `sw.js` so its `VERSION` changes. Installed apps show **Update now** the next time they are online.
